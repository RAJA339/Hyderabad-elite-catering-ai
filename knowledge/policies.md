# Business Policies — Hyderabad Elite Catering

## Booking & Advance
- A quote is valid for 7 days from the date it is sent, or until the price is locked.
- A booking is confirmed only after a 30% advance is received. The balance is due 3 days before the event.
- Price lock: once locked, the per-plate price cannot rise before the event date even if market prices move. A Price Lock certificate with a unique code is issued for bookings above ₹2,00,000.
- Maximum 500 guests per sitting. Larger events are served as two sittings or two dates.

## Cancellation & Refunds
- Cancellation 15 or more days before the event: full refund of the advance.
- Cancellation 7–14 days before: 50% of the advance is refunded.
- Cancellation within 72 hours of the event: advance is non-refundable because ingredients are already procured.
- Date changes are free once if requested at least 7 days in advance and the new date has kitchen capacity.

## Guest Count Changes
- Guest count can be increased any time up to 48 hours before the event, subject to kitchen capacity.
- Guest count can be reduced by up to 10% until 72 hours before the event without charge. Larger reductions are billed at 50% of the per-plate price for the removed plates.

## Service & Setup
- Service staff ratio is 1 server per 25 guests for buffet service, 1 per 15 for sit-down service.
- Live counters need a 3m × 2m space with power. Setup begins 2 hours before service.
- Standard service window is 3 hours; each extra hour is billed per staff member.
- Buffet includes disposable premium plates, cutlery and drinking water. Crockery upgrade is available.

## Food Safety & Dietary
- All non-veg is halal. Veg and non-veg are cooked and served in separate lines with separate utensils.
- Jain menus contain no onion, garlic, potato, carrot, beetroot, radish, ginger or mushroom.
- Allergen information (nuts, dairy, gluten, egg) is provided for every item on request.
- FSSAI licensed kitchen; food is transported in insulated hot boxes and served within 4 hours of cooking.

## Taxes & Payments
- GST of 5% applies on catering services. All quoted per-plate prices are before GST unless marked "incl. GST".
- Payments via UPI, bank transfer, cards and Razorpay payment links. A digital invoice is issued for every payment.

## Data & Privacy (DPDP)
- We store your name, phone, event details and conversation to serve your booking. You can ask us to delete your data any time by replying DELETE MY DATA.
- We send only booking-related messages unless you opt in to festival offers.
