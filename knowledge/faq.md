# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you cater outside Hyderabad?
We serve Hyderabad, Secunderabad and locations within the Outer Ring Road (ORR). Events at farmhouses on Shamshabad, Moinabad and Shankarpally roads are served with a transport charge.

## What is the minimum number of guests?
25 guests for a standard buffet; 50 for menus with live counters; live biryani dum counters need 100+.

## Can I taste the food before booking?
Yes. Tasting sessions for 2–4 people are available for bookings above 150 guests, at our Kompally kitchen, with 3 days' notice.

## How is the price decided?
Every dish is costed from today's Hyderabad wholesale prices for ingredients plus labour and overhead. We show the market benchmark next to our price so you can see the value.

## Why did my per-plate price change from last week?
Prices follow ingredient costs. When chicken, mutton, onion or tomato move sharply, the per-plate price moves a little. Locking your price stops that.

## Do you provide vegetarian and non-vegetarian in the same event?
Yes. Mixed menus have separate cooking and serving lines. We recommend a 60:40 veg:non-veg plate split unless you tell us otherwise.

## Do you do Jain food?
Yes. Our Jain menu has no onion, garlic or root vegetables, and is cooked separately.

## What live counters are available?
Dosa, chaat, pasta, mocktail, dum biryani, tandoor (kebabs and rotis), pani puri, ice cream rolls, and Irani chai.

## Can you serve at my apartment community hall?
Yes. We handle setup, service and cleanup. Please share the association's rules for vehicle entry and kitchen use.

## What is included in the per-plate price?
All food items in the package, service staff, buffet setup, premium disposables and drinking water. Crockery, decor, and extra hours are optional add-ons.

## Do you serve alcohol?
No. We can provide a mocktail counter and bartending-style service for non-alcoholic drinks.

## How far in advance should I book?
2–3 weeks for standard dates; 6–8 weeks for wedding season (November–February, April–June) and major festival weeks.
