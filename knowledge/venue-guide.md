# Venue Guide — Partner Venues & Areas

## Kompally / Medchal
- Sitara Grand Gardens — capacity 500, preferred partner rate, ample parking, power for live counters.
- Sri Lakshmi Function Hall — capacity 300, indoor AC, good for winter weddings.

## Gachibowli / Kokapet / Narsingi
- Palm Meadows Lawn — capacity 400, lawn plus indoor hall, best for engagements and corporate dinners.
- Aakriti Convention — capacity 500, two halls, allows two sittings.

## Secunderabad / Alwal / Sainikpuri
- Vintage Palace — capacity 250, heritage look, popular for half-saree and naming ceremonies.
- Bison Polo Grounds — open air, capacity 500, needs generator (we arrange).

## Jubilee Hills / Banjara Hills
- Taj Krishna banquet — external caterers allowed for select events; premium.
- Rock Garden Terrace — capacity 150, rooftop, ideal for birthdays and small corporate events.

## Tips
- Apartment community halls typically allow 100–200 guests; confirm power supply for live counters.
- For farmhouses beyond ORR, add 45–60 minutes transport; food is kept in insulated hot boxes.
